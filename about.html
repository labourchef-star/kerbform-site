About Kerbform

Kerbform is a small business building paperwork tools for tradespeople and landlords in New Zealand and Australia.

Most compliance paperwork is repetitive: the same questions, the same headings, the same back-and-forth. Our tools ask you the questions that matter for your job and turn your answers into a tidy document.

We keep things simple: no long sign-ups, clear pricing, and plain language. If something is wrong or confusing, tell us and we will fix it.

Our tools help you produce documents. They do not replace advice from a qualified professional, and you are responsible for checking that what you produce suits your job and meets the rules where you work.
