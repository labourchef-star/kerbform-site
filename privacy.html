Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy covers kerbform.com and the tools we run on its subdomains, such as paintswms.kerbform.com and watch.kerbform.com.

What we collect

  • Email address - if you give it to us, for example to receive your document, a receipt, or replies to questions you send.
  • Form inputs - the details you enter into a tool (such as job, site, or hazard details) so we can generate your document.
  • Websites you watch - on watch.kerbform.com, the domains you check or ask us to watch, the results of those checks (certificate and domain expiry, uptime, broken links), and, for agency accounts, client names and logos you add for reports. Domains you check without signing up are cached for a few minutes and not linked to you.
  • IP address - we record it briefly to rate-limit the free checker and stop abuse of reminder sign-ups.
  • Analytics counters - simple counts such as page views and how many documents are generated. We use these to see what is working.
  • Campaign attribution - if you arrive from a link or ad, we store the campaign tags in that link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, and Google's gclid click ID) in a first-party cookie on our own site for 30 days, so our counters can tell which channel visits and sign-ups came from. This is not shared with third parties and we do not use third-party trackers for it.
  • Cookies - small files needed to make the tools work, such as keeping your session or remembering a purchase. We do not use them to sell your information.
  • Payments - purchases are handled by Gumroad. We do not see or store your full card details. Gumroad tells us that a purchase was made and gives us details such as your email address. See Gumroad’s privacy policy.

How we use it

To run the tools, deliver your documents, handle purchases and support, fix problems, and improve the tools. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and retargeting

We do not currently use advertising pixels. We may in future use advertising or retargeting pixels (for example from Meta or Google) to measure ads and show our ads to people who have visited our sites. If we do, we will update this policy. You can opt out of personalised ads through your browser settings, by blocking third-party cookies, through your Google and Meta ad settings, or through industry opt-out pages such as optout.aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.com.

Affiliate links

Some pages may include affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We will clearly mark affiliate links where they appear, and a commission does not change what we recommend.

Storage and retention

Data is stored with our hosting and service providers, which may be outside New Zealand. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 (and the Australian Privacy Act 1988 where it applies) you can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@kerbform.com.
