Simple paperwork tools for tradies and landlords

Kerbform makes small, focused tools for people in New Zealand and Australia who would rather be on the job than filling in forms. Each tool does one thing, gets you a usable document, and gets out of the way.

Tools

PaintSWMS

Build a Safe Work Method Statement for painting jobs. Answer a few questions about the job and get a document you can print or send.

Open PaintSWMS

SiteWatch

Free. Check a website's SSL certificate and domain expiry, whether it's up, and the links on its home page. Get an email before anything runs out. Agencies can watch 25 client sites with weekly link crawls and a branded monthly report.

Open SiteWatch

Agent Loop & Token Burn Checker

Free. Paste a Claude Code or agent session log and see the files it kept re-reading, commands it kept re-running, and roughly how many tokens that cost. Runs in your browser, nothing uploaded.

Open the checker

Kerbform Tenancy

Free. For New Zealand landlords: enter a few tenancy dates and see when the bond has to be lodged, the earliest your rent can go up and when the fixed-term notice window opens, then check what's missing on Healthy Homes.

Open Kerbform Tenancy

Payday Super due dates

Free. For Australian employers: pick a payday and see when super has to reach the fund under the ATO's Payday Super rules, with weekends and state public holidays counted.

Open the checker

Lien Waiver Maker

For US contractors: fill in a conditional or unconditional lien waiver for any state, with statutory wording where the state sets it, and download the PDF.

Open Lien Waiver Maker
